FAQ

Good questions.

How accurate are camera measurements?

Our goal is tailor-level accuracy (within about 1–2 cm) for key measurements in normal lighting. Wearing fitted clothes during the scan gives the best results.

Do you store photos or video of me?

No. The scan runs on your device and only the resulting measurements are saved to your account. You can delete them at any time.

Which phones will it work on?

Any recent iPhone or Android phone with a front or rear camera. No depth sensor is needed.

Can I try clothes from any store?

You can try any garment from Miraal partner brands, and save looks across stores in one wardrobe.

When can I use it?

Miraal is in private beta. Join the waitlist and we'll invite you as soon as a spot opens.
